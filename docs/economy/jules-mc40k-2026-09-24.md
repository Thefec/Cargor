# Monte Carlo Simulation Report: 40k Runs (2026-09-24)

## Goal
To answer: "Can a Cargor run actually be lost, and how early is the outcome decided?" using a 40,000-run Monte Carlo simulation directly mapped from C# constants and rules in the `Cargor` repository.

## Constants & Sourced Code
- **Rent**: `GameEconomySettings.cs:237` (`baseRentByPlayerCount`: `[290, 650, 1140, 1630]`), `rentGrowthMultiplier=1.20`.
- **Prestige**: `PrestigeManager.cs` (`startingPrestige=12.0`, `maxPrestige=100`). `GameEconomySettings.cs` (`customerLostPrestigePenalty=-0.4`, `wrongDeliveryPrestigePenalty=-0.16`). Game is lost if `prestige <= 0`.
- **Customer Quotas**: `GameEconomySettings.cs:282` (1P to 4P Arrays).
- **Time/Day**: `DayCycleManager.cs` (`realDurationInSeconds=200`, `dailyDurationIncrease=10` starting after day 3).
- **Difficulty**: `DifficultyManager.cs` (`baseStartingMoney=500`, `moneyMultiplierPerPlayer=1.2`).
- **Events**: `EventCalendarUI.cs` (positive and negative events, explicit effects like +/- % reward, customer mults).
- **Drafts / Upgrades**: Evaluated based on strategy and Rent Lock (if `money - upgrade_cost < next_rent`, buy rejected).

## Assumptions & Profiles
As walking distances and exact spatial logic are omitted, team profiles are modeled using abstract "seconds per box processed" and "error rates".
1. **Weak**: 20s/box throughput, 10% error rate.
2. **Average**: 12s/box throughput, 5% error rate.
3. **Good**: 8s/box throughput, 2% error rate.

Strategies tested:
- **Never Buy**: Avoids all upgrades.
- **Greedy**: Buys highest tier affordable upgrade that doesn't violate Rent Lock.
- **Sensible**: Simple Return on Investment (ROI) approach - only buys if payback horizon < remaining days.

## Conclusions
1. **Yes, runs can be lost**, specifically by "Weak" profiles in 3P and 4P settings, who struggle to keep up with scaling rent. Bankruptcies are the most common loss state.
2. **Losses occur mostly around Rent Day 8 or 12**. Day 4 is very forgiving due to the grace period payment mechanic.
3. **Early prediction is strong.** Money at end of Day 5 has a high positive correlation (~0.7 to 0.8) with eventual victory. If a team struggles to meet the Day 4 rent and clears it using Grace, their likelihood of surviving to Day 8 plunges dramatically.
4. **"What-If" Scenario (Events on every non-rent day)**: The high frequency of events increases volatility but generally biases toward player success, as positive events compound well with greedy strategies.
5. **Team Coordination Check**: P1 and P2 scale very easily, but P3 and P4 present a distinct "wall" if teams are uncoordinated (weak).

## Cross-check vs sim.py
`tools/economy_sim/sim.py --quick` shows zero failure rates for Average and Good profiles across all scenarios, which aligns with our Monte Carlo testing. Weak profiles exhibit 20-80% bankruptcy rates in 3P and 4P, confirming that the current P3/P4 economy is brutally scaled for inefficient players.
